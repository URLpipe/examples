# URLpipe in LlamaIndex

A short LlamaIndex reader over /markdown: rendered pages in, Documents with their source URL out, ready for an index.

The setup, step by step, is at [https://urlpipe.dev/integrations/llamaindex](https://urlpipe.dev/integrations/llamaindex).

- [`urlpipe_reader.py`](urlpipe_reader.py)
- [`index_pages.py`](index_pages.py) — Index a handful of pages
